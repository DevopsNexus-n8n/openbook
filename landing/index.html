From booking to visit, without the phone calls

Clients book themselves. Openbook does the chasing.

  1. Client books online

    Every business gets its own booking page. Clients pick a service, a person and a time.

  2. Reminders go out

    A reminder is sent before each visit. The client taps to confirm or cancel.

  3. Cancelled slots fill

    If someone cancels, people on your waitlist are offered the time first.
